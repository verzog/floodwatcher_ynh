Flood Watcher is a community flood dashboard for Queensland catchments, starting with the
Brisbane River and the Mary River (the rest are listed as "under construction"). It gathers river heights and rainfall from Brisbane City Council, the Bureau of
Meteorology and the Queensland Government, and shows them on one map.

### Features

- River and creek gauges coloured by trend and flood level, with 3-day graphs.
- Rain gauges with totals for the last hour, since 9 am and longer periods.
- A catchment chooser; BoM flood warnings for each catchment, and gauge alerts worked out from the readings.
- Predictive flood alerts from past floods (lower Lockyer so far), and a "flood event upstream"
  notice on each river gauge.
- Flood maps (state floodplain, council flood areas, past flood extents), flood cameras and
  QLDTraffic road closures.
- Push notifications for chosen gauges and named areas, with no accounts.
- Optional unofficial rain from community weather stations (Weather Underground).

Not an official warning service: always follow the Bureau of Meteorology and emergency services.
